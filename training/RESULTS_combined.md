## Conclusions

**Minutes threshold — keep it token.** Validation picks RS ≥ 50 / PO ≥ 10, and accuracy falls monotonically above it: val RMSE 2.399 at the optimum vs 2.795 at RS ≥ 1000 / PO ≥ 160, where the fit pool has shrunk from 14,235 rows to 4,808. Dropping genuinely degenerate rows helps; dropping real players costs more data than it removes noise. This matches the box and on/off models, which land on the same rung.

**Position one-hot — keep it, but it is close to free.** On validation the two settings differ by 0.001 RMSE, which is noise, and the selector picked `use_pos=False` on that margin. On test, position is worth +0.013 R² on total and +0.011 on defense, and -0.001 on offense. The direction is plausible — position constrains defensive role far more than offensive production — but these gaps are small enough that the honest read is *neutral to slightly positive*. Five columns out of 1143 cost nothing, so they stay in.

Note the test deltas were **not** used to make this choice; they are reported after the fact. Treating a +0.013 test difference as a real effect would be selecting on the held-out set.
